01

The Brief

A full-length Gujarati comedy–drama produced for theatrical release and subsequent Shemaroo platform distribution, demanding end-to-end production management across a three-partner co-production structure with a committed opening date and firm platform delivery obligations.

Ittaa Kittaa presented a production mandate of uncommon complexity for the regional Gujarati market: unite three distinct production entities — Janvi Productions, Gayatri Productions, and Jio Studios — under a single, coherent operational framework, shoot across multiple locations in Gujarat, and deliver a finished feature meeting the exacting technical and editorial standards of both theatrical distribution and Shemaroo's streaming platform — all against a fixed theatrical opening date of 19 January 2024.

02

Background & Organisational Context

Ittaa Kittaa is a 2024 Gujarati family comedy–drama charting the transformation of the Jariwala household after Kavya and Nirav — a middle-class couple confronting infertility — accidentally adopt two sisters, aged seven and thirteen. Written by Antima Pawar and Azhar Saiyed, and directed by the co-directing partnership of Abhinn Sharma and Manthan Purohit, the film tackles cultural stigma around infertility and adoption with warmth, wit, and emotional precision.

Produced under the joint banner of Janvi Productions, Gayatri Productions, and Jio Studios, with Pankaj Keshruwala and Chandni Soni serving as producers, the project sat at the intersection of regional storytelling ambition and national platform infrastructure — a combination that elevated both the operational stakes and the reputational opportunity for every department head involved.

  • Delegated departmental responsibilities to heads of art, costume, camera, and post-production, establishing clear accountability chains and standardised communication protocols across units.
  • Navigated the editorial and technical delivery requirements mandated by Jio Studios and Shemaroo, integrating post-production workflows from picture lock through to final mastering.
  • Oversaw stakeholder management across producers, directors, platform representatives, and local community contacts, maintaining alignment on creative, logistical, and compliance priorities throughout.

The Challenge

Challenge 01
Multi-Stakeholder Contract Management & Co-Production Alignment
Coordinating contractual obligations, creative approvals, and financial reporting across three distinct production partners whilst maintaining unified delivery timelines and editorial governance throughout the production period.
Challenge 02
Location Permitting, Risk Assessment & Local Authority Compliance
Securing multi-location shoot permits across Gujarat, conducting formal risk assessments for public and private filming sites, and managing liaison with municipal bodies, traffic authorities, and cultural site custodians.
Challenge 03
Budget Control & Cashflow Management
Structuring a multi-vendor, multi-location budget with phased drawdowns, managing regional cost variables, and maintaining financial compliance across a co-funded production with a major platform stakeholder.
Challenge 04
Crew Recruitment, Grading & Department Head Onboarding
Sourcing and contracting qualified heads of department within a regional talent pool, standardising call sheets and inter-departmental communication, and managing a multi-unit crew across concurrent shooting days.
Challenge 05
Platform Delivery Compliance & Technical Post-Production Specifications
Meeting Jio Studios' and Shemaroo's precise technical delivery requirements — resolution, subtitle formatting, audio mix specifications, and metadata standards — within a compressed post window tied to a fixed theatrical date.
Challenge 06
Community Engagement, Cultural Sensitivity & Stakeholder Management
Managing relationships with local communities, regional government bodies, and cultural stakeholders across Gujarat, ensuring the production remained operationally respectful, legally compliant, and reputationally sound throughout.
06

Execution & Strategy

01
Unified Co-Production Framework
Formulated a single, consolidated production governance structure binding all three partner entities to agreed approval hierarchies, escalation pathways, and reporting schedules. Eliminated ambiguity over decision-making authority by establishing a centralised production office as the operational hub, through which all contractual, creative, and logistical matters were routed and resolved.
02
Location Strategy & Regulatory Compliance
Built a phased location-clearance programme covering all shooting sites across Gujarat well in advance of principal photography. Produced formal risk assessments for each location, standardised the permit application process across municipal jurisdictions, and assigned dedicated liaison contacts per site — ensuring zero shoot-day disruptions attributable to regulatory non-compliance.
03
Budget Architecture & Financial Controls
Structured a granular, line-by-line production budget with phased financial drawdowns keyed to milestone deliverables. Identified cost-pressure points early through rigorous pre-production cost reporting, allowing the production to maximise resource allocation in high-visibility areas — performance, locations, and music — without exceeding the agreed production envelope of ₹3 crore.
04
Regional Crew Recruitment & Departmental Integration
Led a targeted recruitment drive within Gujarat's regional production talent base, onboarding qualified department heads across camera, art direction, costume, sound, and post-production. Standardised call sheets, inter-departmental briefing documents, and production protocols across all units, creating a coherent operational rhythm the dual-director team could rely on throughout the schedule.
05
Post-Production Pipeline & Platform Compliance
Integrated post-production workflows from picture lock through to technical delivery, building the editorial, colour, audio, and metadata pipeline around Jio Studios' and Shemaroo's delivery specifications from day one. Produced and maintained a platform compliance checklist updated at each post milestone, eliminating late-stage rejections and securing clearance for both theatrical and streaming distribution on schedule.
06
Community Relations & Cultural Production Protocol
Refined the production's community engagement approach in collaboration with local cultural stakeholders across each shooting district. Produced a location-specific code of conduct for all crew, mentored junior production staff on culturally appropriate communication, and maintained active dialogue with regional contacts throughout — building goodwill that materially supported access to key locations and community co-operation.

The Impact

  • Delivered the finished feature on the committed theatrical release date of 19 January 2024 — a non-negotiable distribution milestone met in full, with zero deferral or release compromise.
  • Achieved a ₹3.02 crore gross box office collection — recouping the full production budget in the theatrical window alone, ahead of streaming revenue.
  • Secured an IMDb rating of 7.5 / 10 and a Times of India critical rating of 4 / 5, with reviewers specifically commending concept, direction, music, and performances — validating the standard of execution maintained across all production departments.
  • Completed full technical delivery to Jio Studios' and Shemaroo's platform specifications on schedule, enabling a swift streaming transition following the theatrical run and maximising the film's revenue lifecycle across distribution windows.
  • Produced a Gujarati feature earning positive critical notices across The Times of India, NewzDaddy, FlickOnClick, and Mid-Day Gujarati, establishing the project as a credible benchmark within the contemporary Gujarati film slate.
  • Built a co-production operational model successfully integrating three partner entities — a framework directly applicable to future multi-stakeholder regional productions at equivalent or greater scale.
08

Critical Success Factors & Key Decisions

  • Standardised the co-production governance model before pre-production began — establishing a single operational authority binding all three partners early eliminated contractual ambiguity that would otherwise have surfaced mid-shoot, when the cost of resolution is highest.
  • Planned location compliance as a pre-production deliverable, not a production-phase task — completing formal risk assessments and permit approvals ahead of the shooting schedule created contingency space that protected the programme against unexpected regulatory friction.
  • Built the post-production pipeline around the delivery specification from day one — integrating Jio Studios' and Shemaroo's technical requirements into pre-production planning eliminated the costly rework that frequently undermines regional productions targeting platform release.
  • Identified and retained regional talent with genuine above-the-line capability — investing in quality department heads within the Gujarati talent pool, rather than defaulting to external hires, produced a cohesive, culturally attuned crew whose local knowledge directly accelerated location work and community relations.
  • Maintained budget discipline through milestone-linked drawdowns — structuring financial disbursements against specific production milestones created natural accountability checkpoints and prevented cost drift, keeping the production within its ₹3 crore envelope throughout.
  • Redefined community engagement as a production asset, not a compliance obligation — treating local stakeholder relationships as operationally valuable produced tangible access advantages and contributed directly to the production's ability to achieve its Gujarat-wide location programme.
