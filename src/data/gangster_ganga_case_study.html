The Brief

A Hindi short film produced for the Jio Cinema Film Festival — India's biggest digital film festival — demanding end-to-end executive production management across a two-partner co-production structure, with a fixed streaming premiere date and firm platform delivery obligations to Jio Studios.

Gangster Ganga required delivering a polished, platform-ready Hindi short film within a compressed production schedule, aligning the creative ambitions of director Sahil Rakesh Grover with the exacting technical and editorial standards of Jio Cinema's curated festival slate. The film premiered on 4 October 2023 as one of 20 award-winning titles in the Jio Cinema Film Festival — a prestige streaming event that placed the production alongside the most competitive short-form content in the Indian digital market.

02

Background & Organisational Context

Gangster Ganga is a 2023 Hindi family drama charting the relationship between Ganga — an iron-willed, endearingly unruly grandmother — and Ved, the young man who steps in to care for her in her daughter's absence. Written by Prashant Beybaar and Prativa Archana Rathi, and directed by Sahil Rakesh Grover, the film explores the tensions, warmth, and quiet negotiations between two generations under one roof: a story of self-introspection drawn from nostalgia and the complexity of familial love.

Produced under the joint banner of Janvi Productions and Jio Studios, with Supriya Pathak delivering a standout performance as Ganga alongside Veer Rajwant Singh as Ved, the project sat at the intersection of intimate regional storytelling and national platform infrastructure. Selected for the Jio Cinema Film Festival — a 20-title curated showcase streaming from 29 September 2023 — the film carried both reputational weight and the operational rigour of a premium digital premiere.

Structuring and managing all production expenditure within a tight ₹1 crore budget — maximising on-screen production value whilst maintaining granular financial controls across every department and vendor.

06

Execution & Strategy

01
Festival Delivery Framework
Structured the entire production and post-production schedule around the Jio Cinema Film Festival premiere date, building backwards from the 4 October 2023 deadline. Produced a milestone-driven delivery plan integrating picture lock, colour grade, audio mix, and platform submission into a single, sequenced pipeline — eliminating schedule risk at each post-production stage and securing clearance for the festival slot on time.
02
Budget Architecture & Financial Controls
Formulated a granular, line-by-line production budget within the ₹1 crore envelope, with phased drawdowns keyed to principal photography and post-production milestones. Identified cost-pressure points through rigorous pre-production cost reporting, maximising resource allocation to the areas of highest creative return — performance, production design, and sound — without breaching the agreed financial ceiling.
03
Unified Co-Production Governance
Built a consolidated operational governance structure binding Janvi Productions and Jio Studios to agreed approval hierarchies, reporting schedules, and escalation pathways. Eliminated decision-making ambiguity by establishing a centralised production authority through which all contractual, creative, and logistical matters were resolved — maintaining unified momentum across both partner entities throughout.
04
Talent Management & On-Set Standards
Oversaw all talent engagement across the principal cast, structuring standardised contracts and clearly defined call schedules to support a professional, disruption-free shoot. Produced a compliant, well-organised on-set environment aligned to the requirements of a high-profile cast led by Supriya Pathak — creating the conditions for the quality performance that defined the film's critical reception.
05
OTT Post-Production Pipeline & Technical Compliance
Integrated post-production workflows from picture lock through to final platform submission, building the editorial, colour, audio, and metadata pipeline around Jio Cinema's delivery specifications from day one. Maintained a platform compliance checklist updated at every post milestone — eliminating late-stage technical rejections and ensuring the film was submitted to the festival slate on schedule.
06
Mumbai Location Strategy & Regulatory Compliance
Planned and cleared all Mumbai filming locations well in advance of principal photography, producing formal risk assessments and securing the required municipal permits for each site. Assigned dedicated liaison responsibilities per location, standardised the permit process across all shooting days, and built sufficient contingency into the shooting schedule — producing zero regulatory disruptions throughout the unit's filming programme.

  • Completed full technical delivery to Jio Cinema's platform specifications on schedule, enabling a clean festival premiere and maximising the film's digital reach from launch day.
  • Built an efficient, replicable co-production operational model integrating Janvi Productions and Jio Studios — a framework directly applicable to future short-form and feature productions targeting premium OTT platforms.

Critical Success Factors & Key Decisions

  • Structured the entire production schedule around the festival deadline, not around production convenience — reverse-engineering the shoot and post calendar from the 4 October premiere date created a non-negotiable delivery discipline that kept all departments aligned to a single shared milestone.
  • Built the OTT post-production pipeline from day one — integrating Jio Cinema's technical delivery requirements into pre-production planning eliminated the costly rework that frequently derails short-form productions targeting premium platform release under compressed timescales.
  • Maintained financial discipline through milestone-linked drawdowns — structuring all financial disbursements against specific production milestones created natural accountability checkpoints and prevented cost drift, keeping the production within its ₹1 crore envelope throughout.
  • Standardised the co-production governance model before pre-production commenced — establishing clear operational authority across Janvi Productions and Jio Studios from the outset eliminated contractual ambiguity that would otherwise have surfaced mid-production, when the cost of resolution is highest.
  • Created a professional on-set environment commensurate with the cast's profile — producing compliant, well-organised shoot conditions for a principal cast of Supriya Pathak's calibre was a non-negotiable prerequisite for the standard of performance the film required.
  • Planned location compliance as a pre-production deliverable, not a production-phase task — completing all Mumbai location risk assessments and permit clearances ahead of principal photography created contingency headroom that protected the schedule against regulatory friction.
