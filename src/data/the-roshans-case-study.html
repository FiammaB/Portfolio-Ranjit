01 · The Brief

Seven Decades. Four Episodes. One Mandate.

The Roshans is a four-part Netflix docu-series chronicling the creative lineage of one of Indian cinema's most consequential families — from composer patriarch Roshan Lal Nagrath through to global superstar Hrithik Roshan — across more than seven decades of Bollywood history. Produced by Roshan Creations and released globally on 17 January 2025, the series demanded executive oversight of a year-long production spanning archival rights clearance, high-profile stakeholder access, and full technical delivery compliance to Netflix's international broadcast standards.

With a total production budget of ₹1 Crore INR, this project demonstrates a core professional competency: the ability to plan, manage, and deliver a premium Netflix original to full international platform standards on a lean, tightly controlled budget — eliminating waste, maximising every rupee of production value, and achieving a verified 10% budget saving (₹10 Lacs) without any compromise to creative output or delivery quality.

02 · Background & Organisational Context

The First Authorised Portrait of an Icon.

Roshan Creations commissioned this series as a first-of-its-kind authorised access documentary — granting Netflix and its global audience an intimate, multi-generational portrait of a family that shaped Bollywood across three distinct creative eras. The production integrated 70-plus years of archival materials alongside fresh, candid testimony from family members and senior industry figures including Shah Rukh Khan and Sanjay Leela Bhansali, requiring rigorous intellectual property management, stakeholder co-ordination at the highest level, and delivery against Netflix's exacting global platform standards ahead of a fixed worldwide premiere date.

05 · The Challenges

Seven High-Stakes Operational Problems

A docu-series of this historical scope and commercial profile generated seven distinct high-level production challenges, each requiring structured solutions in line with current UK and international production industry standards:

01

Archival Rights Clearance & Multi-Rights-Holder Licensing Compliance

Sourcing and clearing a substantial volume of archival footage, audio recordings, music compositions, stills, and personal documents held across multiple rights holders — legacy film studios, music labels, private estates, and broadcasters — spanning over seven decades required establishing clear chain of title for each asset and delivering compliant clearance documentation to Netflix's legal and delivery teams.

02

Multi-Stakeholder Access, Talent Scheduling & Subject Management

Securing confirmed interview access with globally recognised, high-demand subjects — including Hrithik Roshan, Shah Rukh Khan, and Sanjay Leela Bhansali — required long-lead stakeholder management, co-ordination across multiple talent representatives and publicists, and the preparation of formal access agreements, NDAs, and editorial approval frameworks, all sustained across a multi-month production schedule.

03

Netflix Deliverables Compliance & Technical Specification Management

Delivering four episodes to Netflix's full global technical specifications — covering approved codecs, audio mixing and mastering standards, subtitle formatting, closed-caption accessibility, audio description requirements, and localisation deliverables for international markets — demanded a clearly structured post-production delivery pipeline established from the outset of pre-production.

04

Location Access, Permitting & Security Risk Assessments Around High-Profile Talent

Filming across private residences, heritage locations, active production studios, and recording facilities in Mumbai — with principal and guest contributors who require personal security provisions, advance recces, and crowd-access management plans — required location-specific risk assessments, formal permissions from facility operators, and structured security co-ordination to protect the safety and privacy of talent on camera.

05

Archival Digitisation, Asset Management & IP Chain of Custody

Integrating rare home video footage, handwritten personal correspondence, family photographs, and unpublished archival audio into a broadcast-grade post-production pipeline required a formal digitisation workflow, verified IP ownership documentation, and a secure digital asset management system maintaining a traceable, audit-ready chain of custody for all materials in line with Netflix's content authentication requirements.

06

Production Insurance Structuring for High-Value, High-Profile Subjects

Structuring an appropriate insurance framework — encompassing cast insurance for globally recognised talent, errors & omissions cover for a production making substantive biographical and historical claims, and full public and production liability — required careful risk underwriting and close co-ordination with specialist entertainment insurers given the sensitivity of personal narratives and the profile of those involved.

07

Streaming & Broadcasting Regulatory Compliance Across Global Release Territories

Ensuring the finished series met Indian streaming regulatory obligations alongside Netflix's international content standards for simultaneous worldwide release — including content classification, I&B Ministry regulatory positioning, and international rating requirements across Netflix's key markets — required proactive compliance planning integrated into the production schedule well in advance of the January 2025 premiere.

06 · Execution & Strategy

Structured Solutions on Every Front

Building a Rights Clearance Infrastructure for Seven Decades of Material

Recognised that a docu-series of this archival breadth could not manage rights on a reactive, asset-by-asset basis. Structured a dedicated clearance framework from pre-production — mapping all anticipated archival material against known rights holders, establishing priority tiers based on editorial centrality, and creating a documentation protocol that tracked each asset from initial identification through to cleared delivery status. Built the clearance workflow in close co-ordination with production counsel and Netflix's legal team, ensuring that every audio recording, film clip, photographic still, and personal document arriving in the editorial suite carried verified chain-of-title documentation before entering the post-production pipeline.

Managing Access to India's Most In-Demand Interview Subjects

Planned talent access as a long-lead critical-path item — building confirmed participation agreements, scheduling frameworks, and editorial approval structures into the production plan from the earliest stages of pre-production rather than treating access as an assumption. Co-ordinated individually across multiple talent management offices, publicists, and personal assistants to lock interview dates well in advance of filming windows, creating schedule redundancy to absorb rescheduling without triggering production delays. Formulated clear parameters for editorial review and sign-off obligations, resolving these contractually before filming commenced to protect post-production timelines.

Secured all talent confirmations — including globally recognised, high-demand contributors — through structured, persistent, and professional follow-up, conducted directly and without third-party intermediary delays. Every date was locked without last-minute disruption, cancellation, or schedule renegotiation, demonstrating the ability to manage high-profile talent access at the highest industry level whilst maintaining productive, trust-based relationships with all parties throughout a year-long production.

Standardising the Netflix Delivery Pipeline Across All Four Episodes

Integrated Netflix's full technical specification requirements into the post-production workflow from picture lock onwards — standardising codec, audio, subtitle, accessibility, and metadata requirements across all four episodes simultaneously rather than addressing them sequentially per episode. Identified the subtitle formatting and audio description obligations for international markets as the highest-risk compliance gap early in post-production, and built additional QC review stages into the delivery schedule to ensure these were resolved before final submission. Eliminated the rework cycles that typically arise when delivery specifications are reviewed only at the point of submission.

Producing Location Risk Assessments & Security Protocols for High-Profile Filming

Produced location-specific risk assessments and method statements for all filming locations across the production — addressing distinct risk profiles for private residential shoots, active studio environments, and any location where public access required active management. Structured security co-ordination plans for filming with talent of Hrithik Roshan's global profile, integrating advance recces, defined access perimeters, and on-set security briefings into the standard call-sheet process. Maintained crew welfare standards and incident escalation protocols throughout the production period.

Establishing Secure Archival Digitisation & Chain of Custody Workflows

Built a formal digitisation protocol governing the intake, cataloguing, and secure handling of all private archival materials entrusted to the production by the Roshan family — including rare home videos, personal correspondence, and unpublished recordings. Created an asset register maintaining clear ownership attribution and access permissions for each item, with an audit-ready record of the material's journey from family custody through digitisation and into the editorial and post-production pipeline. Ensured that all archival materials were returned or archived in line with the agreed terms of access.

Structuring Errors & Omissions and Cast Insurance for Biographical Content

Worked with specialist entertainment insurers to structure an insurance framework calibrated to the specific risk profile of a biographical docu-series — where historical and personal claims carry meaningful E&O exposure, and where the participation of globally recognised talent requires appropriate cast insurance coverage. Integrated the insurance review process into pre-production script and outline sign-off procedures, identifying and resolving potential E&O vulnerabilities before filming rather than managing them retrospectively at the point of delivery.

Navigating Regulatory Compliance for a Simultaneous Global Premiere

Planned regulatory compliance as a pre-production function rather than a post-production correction. Identified the content classification and I&B Ministry positioning requirements for Indian streaming distribution early in the production process, integrating these into editorial decisions rather than addressing them through re-cuts at the point of delivery. Co-ordinated international content rating submissions in line with Netflix's global release schedule, ensuring that all regulatory clearances were secured in parallel with post-production rather than sequentially, protecting the fixed 17 January 2025 premiere date across all territories.

Delivering ₹10 Lacs in Budget Savings on a ₹1 Crore Production

On a production budgeted at ₹1 Crore INR, every operational decision carries direct financial consequence. Identified cost-reduction opportunities across three areas without touching creative output: streamlined vendor contracts through advance planning and consolidated supplier negotiations, eliminating the premium costs that arise from short-notice bookings; refined the shooting schedule to maximise crew productivity per day, eliminating idle time across all filming blocks; and standardised equipment hire and facilities management across the production's multiple Mumbai locations, removing duplication of spend. The combined result was a verified saving of ₹10 Lacs INR — representing a 10% reduction against the approved budget — returned to Roshan Creations at the close of production.

08 · Critical Success Factors & Key Decisions

The Decisions That Held This Production Together

  • Treated archival rights clearance as a pre-production critical-path function, not a post-production problem. Building a dedicated clearance infrastructure from the outset meant that editorial decisions were never constrained by unresolved rights obligations, and that Netflix's legal delivery requirements were met without delay.
  • Planned talent access as a long-lead operational risk from day one. Subjects of this profile do not confirm availability in the short-term window. Building confirmed access agreements, scheduling frameworks, and editorial sign-off structures into the earliest stages of pre-production was the decision that kept the interview schedule intact across a year-long shoot.
  • Integrated Netflix's delivery specifications into post-production from picture lock, not from the point of submission. Standardising technical, accessibility, and localisation requirements across all four episodes simultaneously eliminated the rework and delay cycles that arise when platform compliance is treated as a final-stage task.
  • Resolved E&O and regulatory compliance obligations during pre-production, not after editorial lock. On a biographical production of this scope, managing legal exposure retrospectively risks costly re-cuts and delivery delays. Pre-production resolution of insurance and regulatory frameworks protected both the editorial integrity and the release date.
  • Built schedule contingency around the fixed global premiere rather than treating it as a hard backstop. A simultaneous worldwide Netflix release across 190-plus countries is not a date that moves. Structuring every production phase — clearances, access, post-production, compliance, insurance — backwards from 17 January 2025 meant that contingency was built in, not borrowed from.
